## Known gotchas

1. **Statements are treated as untrusted even though they originate inside
   the firm** — the explicit framing is "pre-generated by an upstream system
   you don't control" (`statement-auditor.md:19`). This is a strong
   safety-by-default posture.
2. **LP statement contents are NPI** for individual LPs (NAV, contribs,
   distribs per LP). Reg S-P safeguarding applies to flagger output and
   any logging.
3. **NAV pack is the source of truth** — must be locked at audit time;
   provenance + hash capture required.
4. **"Pass/hold recommendation; IR distributes after sign-off" invariant**
   at line 26 is prose-only.
5. **Conditional Emerald relevance** — same as `valuation-reviewer`: only
   applicable if Emerald operates a private fund.
